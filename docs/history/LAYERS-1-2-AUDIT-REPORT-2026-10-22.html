Layers 1 and 2 Audit

The foundation and the links-and-properties layer of the DB Refinements plugin, checked on 22 October 2026 at versions 0.4.272 to 0.4.279, in the Illness Test Graph.

Yes

Layers 1 and 2 are done. Every feature of the two layers works in Logseq, in light and dark mode; the parts are cleanly separated; nothing the plugin does is slow or moves the page. Seven small bugs found on the way are fixed and pushed (0.4.273 to 0.4.279).

Five things are yours to decide (below). None of them stops layer 3 from starting; the first one (splitting the tag map's file) is best done before it.

For you to decide

  1. 1. Split the tag map's file before layer 3. tags.js is 2,339 lines with about twenty jobs, the same problem the proposals had before their split. Recommendation: three files, the way the proposals were split: the graph-changing actions (merge, choice into child tag, turn into links, with their undos), the property map and its edit boxes, and the tree itself. Example: today a change to how #Disease is dragged and a change to how Confirmation Status's choices are edited live in the same file.
  2. 2. A tidy-up pass on the README and the code. Line 5 still says only the map and the rows run; 75 of the 87 rows in "Every setting" are resting settings with nothing marking them, and four live ones (Proposals while you write, Hide empty properties, Only in these graphs, Foundation only) are missing. With it, one housekeeping commit that changes nothing on screen: take() moved into shared.js (eight identical copies), six unused imports in main.js, the empty hideToast(), the one-off "tagTreeReinstated" switch and the "for a minute" wording dropped. Recommendation: a "What runs now" part with the six live parts and their 16 settings, a "Resting" part for the rest, and the commit.
  3. 3. The ring on any capsule, in every graph. Since 0.4.270 the ring also shows on a capsule Logseq's own Left/Right put the keyboard on, in every graph, not only the graphs the proposals may write in. Recommendation: keep it (it only shows where the keyboard is, and Logseq shows nothing there itself), but say so in the README.
  4. 4. What Escape's clearing means. After Escape, the proposals come back only when you leave the bullet and enter it again; typing on does not bring them back. The README says "until you next edit the bullet". Recommendation: keep the behaviour and change the words, since bringing them back on the next letter would undo the Escape you just pressed.
  5. 5. Guessing choices such as Status. On the Illness Test Graph the vote gets Status right 7 times and wrong 2 (both expectations that changed since). On the 31,000-bullet scratch graph, whose statuses are random, it is wrong 255 times, and that scorer now fails its limit. Recommendation: keep the choice guesses (a guess is dotted and only Enter takes it), and raise the scratch graph's limit, since random statuses cannot be predicted by anything; if you would rather not see Status guessed at all, say so and it goes back to 0.4.264's behaviour.
